# Paging

## Overview

**Placeholder spec — not yet designed.**

The Paging feature will handle Backlog work item lists that grow beyond a practical display limit (see `spec.backlog.md`). The paging mechanism — virtual scroll, page-based navigation, or a load-more button — is TBD and will be decided when item counts at real usage warrant it.

## Out of Scope for MVP

Everything — paging is explicitly out of scope for the MVP Backlog. `localStorage` item counts are expected to remain small in early usage, and no row limit is enforced.
